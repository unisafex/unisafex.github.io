Support & Grievance

How to report an app, booking, payment, privacy, safety, or content issue and track its resolution.

Effective 17 September 2026 | Version 1.0

Not an emergency channel: call 112 in India for immediate danger. For cyber financial fraud, call 1930. Do not wait for an email reply.

1. Support channels

Signed-in app users can create and track a ticket in Help & Support. You can also email abhishek.work962511@gmail.com. Include a clear title, category, device and app version, order or transaction reference when relevant, and the result you are requesting.

2. What not to send

Never send passwords, private keys, full payment-card details, CVV, PIN, or one-time passwords. Redact passport numbers, bank details, and unrelated personal information from screenshots.

3. Handling targets

UniSafeX aims to acknowledge a support or grievance report within 48 hours and work toward resolution within 30 days. Complex cases involving an external seller, payment provider, transport operator, or network participant may require more time; status updates will be provided where the ticket system supports them.

4. Privacy and account requests

Use the Privacy or Account category for access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, or account-security requests. Identity verification may be required before personal data is disclosed or deleted.

5. Booking and payment issues

For an external partner order, contact that provider first and share its case reference with UniSafeX. For a future in-app or ONDC order, use the order-linked support action so the correct seller and transaction can be identified.

6. Escalation

If you are not satisfied, reply to the existing ticket and request grievance review. Consumers in India may also contact the National Consumer Helpline at 1915 or 1800-11-4000. These external options do not replace emergency, police, court, regulator, or provider-specific remedies.

7. Emergency and safety reports

UniSafeX support is not monitored as an emergency channel. For immediate danger in India call 112. For cyber financial fraud call 1930. Report inaccurate safety information after contacting the appropriate authority when urgent.

8. Current operator disclosure

The UniSafeX project team currently handles support through the email address above during the development and pilot stage. The registered legal entity name, business address, formal grievance officer details, and jurisdiction-specific disclosures must be added before paid commercial launch.

9. Official resources

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 on India Code
  • Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 on MeitY
  • Consumer Protection rules on the Department of Consumer Affairs website
  • National Consumer Helpline

Contact UniSafeX

Email abhishek.work962511@gmail.com. Reuse the same email thread or app ticket when escalating so the history stays together.
