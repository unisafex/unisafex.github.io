Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your UniSafeX account and understand what may need to be retained.

Effective 17 September 2026 | Version 1.0

Use the account email: this helps UniSafeX verify the request without asking for passwords, payment secrets, or full identity documents.

1. Start a deletion request

If you can sign in, open Settings, choose Help & Support, and submit a ticket with the Account deletion category. If you cannot sign in, email abhishek.work962511@gmail.com from the address linked to the account. Use the subject "UniSafeX account deletion request".

2. Identity verification

UniSafeX may ask you to confirm control of the account email or provide limited information needed to locate the account. Never send a password, one-time password, full payment-card number, PIN, CVV, private key, or full identity-document number.

3. What deletion covers

After verification, UniSafeX will delete or de-identify the account profile and personal data it controls, including saved preferences and content that is not required for an active dispute, security investigation, transaction record, or legal obligation. Public reviews and replies will be removed or de-identified where appropriate.

4. Data held by other providers

Deleting a UniSafeX account does not cancel or delete a booking, ticket, payment, guide request, or account completed with an external provider. Contact that provider directly under its cancellation and privacy terms.

5. Retention exceptions

Limited records may be retained when required for law, tax, accounting, payment reconciliation, fraud prevention, safety, security, or an unresolved complaint. Retained data is restricted to the relevant purpose and removed or de-identified when that purpose ends.

6. Processing and confirmation

UniSafeX aims to acknowledge the request within 48 hours and complete verified requests within 30 days unless a lawful exception or complex provider dependency applies. The verified contact channel will be used to confirm completion or explain any retained records.

Request account deletion

Email abhishek.work962511@gmail.com from the account address, or use the signed-in Help & Support ticket form in the app.
