Cancellation & Refund

How cancellations, failed payments, partner bookings, guide requests, and future UniSafeX or ONDC orders are handled.

Effective 17 September 2026 | Version 1.0

Start with the confirmation: the company that accepted the payment or issued the ticket normally controls cancellation and refund processing.

1. Identify where the booking was completed

Check the confirmation screen, email, ticket, or payment statement. If UniSafeX opened a partner website or app and the purchase was completed there, that partner controls the booking and its cancellation or refund.

2. External partner bookings

Request cancellation directly from the hotel, transport operator, attraction, guide platform, payment provider, or other partner that accepted the order. Its fare rules, deadlines, fees, no-show rules, taxes, and refund timing apply. UniSafeX support may help identify the correct channel but cannot cancel or refund an order it does not control.

3. Guide requests

A guide request is not a confirmed paid booking unless the app clearly shows confirmation, provider details, price, and payment status. To withdraw an unconfirmed request, contact support. Confirmed guide services follow the cancellation terms shown before payment or confirmation.

4. Demo bookings

Demo orders, demo fares, and demo tickets do not charge money and are not valid for travel. Cancelling a demo order only changes its local demo state; no refund is created.

5. Future UniSafeX or ONDC orders

When a real in-app order is available, the applicable seller terms, cancellation window, cancellation charge, refund eligibility, and payment collector will be shown before confirmation. A cancellation request is complete only when the order status confirms cancellation.

6. Refund method and timing

An approved refund will normally return through the original payment method unless the provider or law requires another method. The order screen or provider will show the expected processing period. Banks and payment systems may require additional time after a refund is initiated.

7. Failed, pending, or duplicate payments

Do not pay repeatedly while a payment is pending. Keep the transaction reference and bank record. Report an unexplained debit or duplicate charge to the payment provider and through UniSafeX support. Never send a full card number, PIN, password, CVV, or one-time password.

8. Disputes

Include the order or transaction reference, provider, date, amount, payment status, and requested resolution. UniSafeX may need to coordinate with the seller, payment collector, or network participant. Consumer rights available under applicable law remain unaffected.
