Community Guidelines

Rules for reviews, replies, ratings, photos, and other contributions shared by travelers and the UniSafeX team.

Effective 17 September 2026 | Version 1.0

Useful contributions are firsthand, relevant, and respectful. Review images and other content may wait for moderation before they become public.

1. Be truthful and relevant

Reviews and replies should describe a genuine travel experience and the place or service being reviewed. Do not submit fabricated experiences, coordinated ratings, advertising, repeated content, or reviews offered in exchange for undisclosed benefits.

2. Respect people and safety

Do not post threats, harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, instructions for wrongdoing, graphic harm, or content that creates a real-world safety risk. Criticism is allowed when it is specific, factual, and respectful.

3. Protect privacy

Do not publish passport details, phone numbers, home addresses, payment information, precise private locations, private conversations, or identifiable images of people without permission. Avoid showing children unless a parent or lawful guardian has agreed.

4. Photos and rights

Upload only images you created or are permitted to share. Images may be cropped or compressed for display and may wait for moderation. Do not upload misleading, illegal, copyrighted, watermarked, or unrelated media.

5. Replies and official identity

Users may reply to reviews but may not impersonate the reviewer, a business, an administrator, or the UniSafeX team. Verified team or admin replies may use a highlighted label. A highlighted reply does not erase the original review or guarantee the underlying service.

6. Moderation

UniSafeX may hold, hide, reject, label, limit, or remove content that violates these guidelines, applicable law, safety requirements, or product integrity. Automated signals may assist review, but users can request human review through support.

7. Reporting and appeals

Report content with its place name, review details, reason, and supporting context. The author or affected user may appeal a moderation decision through Support & Grievance. Repeated or serious violations may lead to account restrictions.
