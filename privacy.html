Privacy Policy

How UniSafeX handles account, profile, location, community, support, partnership, and booking-related information.

Effective 17 September 2026 | Version 1.0

Pilot notice: UniSafeX is currently a development and pilot service. Features marked Demo, including the ONDC Metro demo, do not create a real booking, payment, ticket, or network transaction.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the UniSafeX mobile app and website. It explains what personal data may be processed, why it is used, when it may be shared, and how to request access, correction, or deletion.

2. Information you provide

Depending on the feature you use, we may process:

  • Account information such as email address, user ID, authentication status, and profile photo.
  • Optional travel-profile information including name, gender, nationality or country, preferred city, passport country, visa type, visa expiry, and travel purpose.
  • Saved places, reviews, ratings, replies, uploaded images, guide requests, support tickets, and moderation appeals.
  • Partnership form details such as organization, contact person, contact method, region, website, and proposal.
  • Booking or support references you choose to provide.

Do not submit passport numbers, passwords, private keys, payment PINs, CVV, or one-time passwords.

3. Location and permissions

With your permission, precise or approximate location may be used to show nearby places, estimate distance, choose a ride pickup, and display routes. You can deny location access and enter a place manually where supported. Camera, photo, notification, and speech permissions are used only for the feature you choose and remain controllable through your device settings.

4. Booking and transaction data

External partner links may include route, date, passenger count, or destination information needed to open the requested search. If real in-app or ONDC booking is enabled later, UniSafeX may process search, quote, order, seller, ticket, payment-status, cancellation, refund, and support metadata required to complete and support the transaction. Secret payment credentials should be handled by an approved payment provider, not stored in the app.

5. How information is used

  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Maintain profiles, saved places, preferences, and travel tools.
  • Provide maps, route planning, guide requests, booking handoffs, and support.
  • Publish and moderate reviews, replies, ratings, and approved photos.
  • Prevent misuse, investigate security events, and improve reliability.
  • Meet legal, safety, fraud-prevention, and dispute obligations.

UniSafeX does not sell personal profile data.

6. Public content

Reviews, display names, approved images, ratings, and replies may be visible publicly. Images can be held for team approval. Admin or team replies may be highlighted. We may hide or remove content under the Community Guidelines. Account deletion requests may not require removal of records that must be kept for law, security, fraud prevention, or a live dispute.

7. Service providers and sharing

UniSafeX uses service providers such as Supabase for authentication, database and storage, and Google services for maps and routes. The website partnership form uses FormSubmit to deliver submissions by email. A booking, guide, transport, payment, or future ONDC participant may receive the limited information needed for the action you request. Each third party has its own privacy terms.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect a person from harm, to investigate fraud or security, or during a lawful business reorganization.

8. Retention and security

Account and profile data is kept while the account is active and as needed for the service, security, disputes, and legal obligations. Booking, payment, refund, and support records may require longer retention under provider rules or applicable law. UniSafeX uses access controls, Row Level Security, restricted administration, and server-side secrets, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You may update available profile fields in the app and control device permissions. You may request access, correction, completion, deletion, consent withdrawal, or grievance review by using in-app Help & Support or the contact below. We may verify identity before acting and may retain information when legally required or necessary for a fraud, safety, payment, or transaction dispute.

10. Children and international processing

An account should be created only by someone able to provide valid consent. A child must use the service with a parent or lawful guardian. Service providers may process data in India or other locations where they operate, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

11. Changes

Material changes will be posted with a revised effective date. Where law requires renewed notice or consent, UniSafeX will request it before the affected processing begins.
